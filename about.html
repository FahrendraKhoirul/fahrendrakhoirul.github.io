What I'm about.

A software engineer from Malang who enjoys the full shape of a product: the messy first thought, the architecture, and the thing that finally ships.

Where I'm from

Malang, Indonesia.

I grew up in Malang — a city in East Java that's just the right size. Big enough to have a tech scene, small enough that you can still think. I started coding here, and I still live here.

What I do

I build software from idea to production.

I started with Flutter and mobile development, but I can't stay in one lane. I care about understanding the whole system — why something is being built, who it's for, how the architecture works, and how it ships.

Right now I work across mobile, backend, and AI/ML. I publish on pub.dev, write on Medium, and push code on GitHub.

How I think

Simple, thoughtful, well-designed.

I believe good systems are simple, make sense, and are well-designed — not complicated because of "best practices" without reason. I care about clean architecture, good UX, and understanding the full picture.

I also play piano. Architecture is the score. Every line of code is a note. Clean code is harmony.

What I'm looking for

Interesting problems with good people.

I want to work on things that matter — products that solve real problems, with teams that care about craft. I'm interested in building things that work well and make sense.
