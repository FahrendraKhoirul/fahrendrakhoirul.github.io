What I'm about.

Where I'm from

Malang, Indonesia.

I grew up in Malang — a city in East Java that's just the right size. Big enough to have a tech scene, small enough that you can still think. I started coding here, and I still live here.

What I do

I build software from idea to production.

I started with Flutter and mobile development, but I can't stay in one lane. I care about understanding the whole system — why something is being built, who it's for, how the architecture works, and how it ships. I enjoy turning messy ideas into structured, working products.

Right now I work across mobile, backend, and AI/ML. I've built SaaS platforms, healthcare apps, open source packages, and small tools that solve my own problems. I publish on pub.dev, write on Medium, and push code on GitHub.

How I think

Simple, thoughtful, well-designed.

I believe good systems are simple, make sense, and are well-designed — not complicated because of "best practices" without reason. I care about clean architecture, good UX, and understanding the full picture rather than just writing code.

I also play piano. Not classical — just piano. There's something about the way music requires both structure and feel that maps directly to how I think about software. Architecture is the score. Every line of code is a note. Clean code is harmony.

What I'm looking for

Interesting problems with good people.

I want to work on things that matter — products that solve real problems, with teams that care about craft. I'm not interested in technology for technology's sake. I'm interested in building things that work well and make sense.

If that sounds like your kind of project, get in touch.
